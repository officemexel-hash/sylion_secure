## Rules

Separate certifiable baseline from `[A]` autonomous modules. Do not claim impossible security, anonymity, or PHANTOM certification. Replace absolute claims with controls, evidence, and residual risks.

Use `HUMAN GATE REQUIRED` when legal interpretation, certification scope, or customer-facing security claims are uncertain.

## Output

Compliance verdict, risky wording, safer replacement wording, required evidence, approval path, human gate owner.
