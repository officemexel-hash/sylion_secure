# SYLION Legal And Safety Boundaries

Use this reference whenever work touches PHANTOM, radio identifiers, stealth, jurisdictional rotation, lawful intercept risks, evasion, or destructive operations.

## Baseline Separation

SYLION baseline is a certifiable secure communication platform. PHANTOM is autonomous `[A]`, outside the certifiable core.

Do not blend PHANTOM behavior into baseline product claims, installers, compliance scope, or default configuration.

## Allowed Work

Allowed:

- Defensive architecture.
- Threat modeling.
- Residual-risk documentation.
- Compliance boundaries.
- Policy controls.
- High-level operational constraints.
- Safe router hardening.
- Auditability and incident response.
- Legal review prompts and exception records.

## Restricted Work

Do not provide operational instructions that enable illegal surveillance evasion, identity falsification, unauthorized access, or destructive cover-up.

Treat these as legal-review or redaction zones:

- IMEI spoofing or rotation.
- Unauthorized IMSI manipulation.
- Bypassing lawful network controls.
- Stealth transport meant to defeat jurisdictional restrictions.
- Evidence destruction outside approved incident response and lawful data-retention policy.
- Instructions to hide from law enforcement or regulators.

When a document contains such content, a SYLION skill should:

1. Label it as PHANTOM `[A]` or legal-review material.
2. Avoid turning it into implementation steps.
3. Recommend counsel/CISO approval.
4. Preserve only defensive, compliance, or risk-language in baseline artifacts.
5. Trigger `HUMAN GATE REQUIRED` before any final architecture, product, or implementation decision.

## Honest Capability Statement

SYLION should never claim perfect invisibility or impossible security. It should claim constrained blast radius, isolation, encryption, auditable controls, and explicitly documented residual risks.
